Shipping policy

All orders are processed within 1–3 business days after confirmation. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed on the next working day.

Delivery time

Delivery within India usually takes 7–8 business days after dispatch. Remote locations may take longer depending on courier availability.

Shipping charges

Shipping charges are paid by the customer and will be confirmed before the order is accepted.

Tracking and delays

Tracking details are shared through WhatsApp, SMS or email where available. Courier delays, weather, public holidays and unforeseen circumstances may affect delivery; we will assist with tracking.

Incorrect address

Please provide accurate contact and delivery details. Additional charges may apply for re-shipping due to an incorrect address.

Returns and refunds

Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for damaged, defective or incorrect products, subject to verification. Contact us as soon as possible through WhatsApp or email with your order details and supporting photos.

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
